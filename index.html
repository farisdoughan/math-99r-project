What exactly is being calculated?

The image is resized while preserving its aspect ratio, then converted to grayscale using 0.2126R + 0.7152G + 0.0722B, rounded to integers from 0 to 255. Transparent pixels are composited over white. Coordinates are zero-based: x increases to the right and y increases downward.

The scan includes every pixel, row by row, from top left to bottom right. At image boundaries, the nearest edge pixel is repeated (replicate padding). The shown matrices are applied directly, without flipping: technically this is cross-correlation, a common convention for these operators.

Sobel responses are raw weighted sums. For display, magnitude is scaled against the largest magnitude in this image. Signed responses use a diverging scale: blue is negative, white is zero, and orange is positive; brightness alone does not encode their magnitude. The calculation always shows unscaled values. No thresholding, thinning, or Gaussian preprocessing is applied.
